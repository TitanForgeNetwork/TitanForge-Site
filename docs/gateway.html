DOCUMENTATION

Gateway (planned)

A planned way to move assets from Ethereum-family chains into private TFN, and back, straight from your TitanForge wallet. It is planned to start with ETH and USDC on Base, with Robinhood Chain to follow. It is not built yet, and it comes after mainnet.

TitanForge is in development and no TFN coin exists. This page describes a feature that is planned, not software you can use. Until the gateway is built and audited, everything on this page is a plan.

01

What it is

The gateway is planned as part of the TitanForge wallet. Ethereum-family chains are the blockchains that run Ethereum’s smart-contract system. You choose an asset you hold on one of them and receive private TFN for it in your own wallet. The way back works in reverse: you send TFN from your wallet and receive the asset at an address of yours on that chain.

You need no exchange account.

Public, not private

The other chain stays public. Your address there, the asset, the amount and the time of each transfer can be seen by anyone on that chain. The gateway brings value into private TFN; what happens on the other chain stays public.

02

What is planned

The gateway is being designed under these conditions.

No custodian
Nobody holds your funds in between.
Screened addresses
Every incoming address is screened before anything moves, for example against sanctions lists. An address that fails the check cannot use the gateway.
No admin key
No admin key can change the gateway’s rules or pause it, and the team holds none.
No gateway token
There is no token to buy or hold in order to use it.
Tungsten later
Tungsten, the planned private stablecoin, is off at mainnet launch. Once it is active, it comes through the same gateway.
Checks first
It handles real funds only after mainnet, independent security audits and a legal review.

03

Where it stands

The plan so far
PointNow
Which chains come firstBase, then Robinhood Chain once a legal review of its terms allows it. The companies that run these chains can block addresses or transactions on them: that power is theirs, not the project’s. Other Ethereum-family chains may follow, each with its own contract.
Which assets come firstETH and USDC. USDC can be frozen by its issuer, Circle: that power is Circle’s, not the project’s.
How it works in detailBeing designed. The design, its cryptography and its legal basis are still to be reviewed.
A dateNone is set.

04

When

After mainnet. Before the gateway handles real funds, it needs independent security audits and a legal review. Nothing of it is built yet, and no date is set.
